Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 7, 2026  •  Last Updated: June 12, 2026 (v1.1)

CoachConnect Pro is used by youth baseball teams and collects information about children. We take children's privacy seriously. Section 4 explains our practices regarding children under 13 in detail.

1. Who We Are

CoachConnect Pro ("CoachConnect Pro," "we," "us," or "our") is a youth baseball team management application operated by Coach Connect Pro, LLC, a limited liability company organized in the State of Arkansas, located at 10 Gilley Lane, Conway, AR 72032.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to our website at coachconnectpro.net and our application (collectively, the "Service"). If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information from Account Holders (Coaches and Parents)

When a coach or parent creates an account, we collect:

  • Name (full name)
  • Email address
  • Account role (coach or parent) and the team you are associated with
  • Authentication credentials (managed securely by our authentication provider; we do not store your raw password)

2.2 Information About Players (Children)

Coaches and parents enter information about players on a team. This may include:

  • First name or nickname (the Service asks coaches and parents to enter a first name or preferred name only — not a full legal name)
  • Age and age group
  • Jersey number and primary position
  • Batting and throwing handedness
  • Coach notes and player goals
  • Skill assessments recorded over time (e.g., dated ratings of hitting, fielding, etc.)
  • Drill assignments, completion status, and coach feedback
  • Game statistics and lineup/field-position placements
  • Photos and videos of the player uploaded by a coach or parent

2.3 Communications

In-app messages and chat content, including any attachments shared between coaches and parents within the Service.

2.4 Payment Information (Coaches Only)

When a coach subscribes to a paid plan, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not collect or store full payment card numbers. Stripe provides us with limited subscription information (such as plan type, subscription status, and a customer identifier) needed to manage your account. Stripe's handling of your payment data is governed by Stripe's own privacy policy.

2.5 Technical and Usage Information

  • Push notification tokens, if you opt in to notifications, so we can deliver alerts about messages, schedules, and assignments.
  • Standard technical data generated by hosting and infrastructure providers (such as log data) needed to operate and secure the Service.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • Let coaches manage rosters, schedules, lineups, practice plans, and player development
  • Let parents view their own child's assignments, schedule, progress, and communications
  • Send notifications you have opted into (messages, schedule changes, assignments)
  • Process and manage coach subscriptions
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Service
  • Respond to support requests and communicate with you about your account
  • Comply with our legal obligations

We do not sell personal information. We do not use children's personal information for behavioral or targeted advertising, and we do not allow third parties to do so through our Service.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

This section describes how we handle the personal information of children under the age of 13, in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA") and the FTC's COPPA Rule.

4.1 We Knowingly Collect Children's Information

CoachConnect Pro is designed to help coaches and parents manage youth baseball teams, and we knowingly collect personal information about players who may be under 13. Because of this, we apply COPPA protections to all player information unless we have a reasonable basis to treat a player as 13 or older.

4.2 Parental Consent Is Required

We require verifiable parental consent before a child's personal information is collected or made available through the Service.

In practice:

  • A parent must create a parent account and provide consent during onboarding before any player information linked to their child is accessible to them through the Service.
  • The consent step clearly identifies what information is collected, how it is used, and who it is shared with, and links to this Privacy Policy.
  • We retain a dated record that consent was given.

A parent may refuse to consent. If consent is refused or later withdrawn, we will not collect further personal information from the child and will delete the child's information as described in Section 4.6.

4.3 What We Collect About Children and Why

We collect only the information reasonably necessary for a coach and parent to manage a youth player's participation and development on a team. As described in Section 2.2, this includes the player's name, age/age group, jersey number, position, handedness, coach notes and goals, skill assessments, drill assignments and feedback, game statistics, lineup placements, and any photos or videos uploaded about the player.

We collect this information so coaches can organize the team, plan practices and lineups, and track player development, and so parents can follow their own child's schedule, assignments, and progress. We do not condition a child's participation on the collection of more information than is reasonably necessary for these activities.

4.4 We Do Not Use Children's Data for Advertising

We do not use children's personal information for targeted or behavioral advertising, and we do not disclose it to third parties for advertising purposes. We do not sell children's personal information.

4.5 How We Share Children's Information

Children's information is accessible within the Service only to the coach(es) of the player's team and to the player's own parent/guardian account. We share children's information with our service providers (described in Section 5) only as needed to operate the Service — for example, to store data and media securely. We require these providers to protect the information and use it only to provide services to us. We obtain separate parental consent before disclosing a child's personal information to any third party for a purpose other than providing the Service.

4.6 Parental Rights: Review and Deletion

As a parent or guardian, you have the right to:

  • Review the personal information we have collected about your child
  • Refuse to permit further collection or use of your child's information
  • Request deletion of your child's personal information

To exercise these rights, contact us at coachconnectpro@yahoo.com. When you request deletion, we will delete the player's personal information from our active systems and storage — including the player record, assignments, skill assessments, game statistics, lineup placements, and any uploaded photos and videos — and we will confirm when this is complete. We may need to verify that you are the child's parent or guardian before acting on a request.

4.7 Data Minimization and Retention for Children

We retain children's personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service and fulfill the purposes described above, in accordance with our written Data Retention Policy. We do not retain it indefinitely. Specific retention periods are set out in Section 6. When information is no longer needed, or upon a valid deletion request, we delete it as described in Section 6.

4.8 Teens Ages 13–16

We extend the protections in this Section 4 to players ages 13–16, consistent with the Arkansas Children and Teens' Online Privacy Protection Act. For these players: (a) we collect the same categories of information, for the same purposes, described in Section 4.3; (b) consent may be provided by the teen's parent or guardian (our standard flow) or by the teen; (c) we do not collect or use their personal information for targeted advertising, and we do not permit others to; and (d) teens and their parents have the same rights to access, correct, and delete information described in Sections 4.6 and 8. We apply these protections to all players in this age range regardless of where they live.

5. How We Share Information / Service Providers

We share information only as described below. We do not sell personal information. We use the following trusted service providers ("processors") to operate the Service. They process information on our behalf and are contractually limited to that purpose:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and stores uploaded files, including player photos and videos.
  • Stripe — processes coach subscription payments.
  • Vercel — hosts and serves the application.
  • Web push notification services — deliver notifications you opt into.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights, safety, or property of CoachConnect Pro or others, or in connection with a business transfer (such as a merger or acquisition), subject to the protections of this Policy and applicable law.

We do not collect, use, disclose, or permit others to collect personal information from children or teens for targeted advertising, and we do not display third-party advertising in the Service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We maintain a written Data Retention Policy. In summary:

  • Player data: retained while the player is on an active roster. When a coach deletes a player, or a parent/guardian makes a verified deletion request, the player's records, photos, and videos are removed from our active database and file storage immediately, as described in Section 4.6.
  • Accounts: you may request deletion of your account by contacting coachconnectpro@yahoo.com. Verified requests are completed within 30 days.
  • Inactive accounts: accounts with no sign-in for 24 consecutive months are deleted following 30 days' email notice.
  • Consent records: retained for 3 years after the related account is deleted, as evidence of compliance.
  • Backups: deleted information may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period (currently up to approximately 35 days) before aging out in the ordinary course of operations, and is not restored except for disaster recovery.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information, including access controls that restrict each team's data to that team, encrypted connections, and securely managed credentials. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to access, correct, or delete it. To make a request, contact us at coachconnectpro@yahoo.com. Parental rights regarding children's information are described in Section 4.6.

You can also:

  • Update your account information within the Service
  • Opt out of push notifications through your device or browser settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes — especially to how we handle children's information — we will take reasonable steps to notify account holders and, where required, obtain renewed parental consent. The "Last Updated" date above reflects the most recent version.
