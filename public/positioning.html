Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. The positioning trainer is free with no account and no login. Your progress saves in your own browser on this device. CoachConnect Pro — the full team-management app — is a separate paid product with a free trial.

What ages is this for?

The plays use standard nine-player defense, which shows up around Little League Majors, travel ball, and school ball. Younger teams can use it one position at a time with the position filter.

How should I use it at practice?

Put a situation on the screen, have the players call out their job out loud before you press play, then animate it and check. Two minutes per situation in the dugout beats twenty minutes of standing around on the field.

What are the four jobs?

On every batted ball each defender is doing exactly one of four things: getting the ball, covering a base, cutting a long throw so it can be redirected, or backing up a throw in case it gets away. Nine players, four jobs, every single pitch — if a kid is standing still watching the play, he is out of position.
