Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 7, 2026

CoachConnect Pro is designed for youth baseball coaches and the parents of their players. We take the privacy of children seriously. Children do not create accounts — only coaches and parents do.

1. Who We Are

CoachConnect Pro ("we," "us," or "our") is a software application that helps youth baseball coaches manage their teams, assign drills, track player development, and communicate with parents. We are operated as an independent product.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use CoachConnect Pro:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Team and roster information: Team name, player names, ages, positions, jersey numbers, and batting/throwing preferences entered by coaches.
  • Parent contact information: Parent names, phone numbers, and email addresses entered by coaches.
  • Player performance data: Drill assignments, completion status, coach notes, skill snapshots, and goals entered by coaches and parents.
  • Media: Photos and videos of players uploaded by coaches or parents for development purposes.
  • Messages: In-app messages between coaches and parents.
  • Usage data: How you interact with the app, including features used and session duration.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and provide the CoachConnect Pro service
  • Enable coaches to manage rosters and track player development
  • Allow parents to view their child's assigned drills and progress
  • Facilitate communication between coaches and parents
  • Improve and maintain the app
  • Respond to support requests
  • Send important service-related communications

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

CoachConnect Pro complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Children under the age of 13 do not create accounts on our platform. Player information is entered and managed by coaches and parents — adult account holders — on behalf of children.

Coaches are responsible for obtaining appropriate consent from parents before entering player information into the app. Parents may request the deletion of their child's information at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with advertisers. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Within your team: Coaches can see all player and parent information for their team. Parents can see their own contact information and their linked child's progress.
  • Service providers: We use Supabase to store your data securely. They process data on our behalf and are contractually bound to protect it.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's secure cloud infrastructure. We use row-level security to ensure coaches and parents can only access data belonging to their team. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

7. Media (Photos and Videos)

Photos and videos uploaded to CoachConnect Pro are stored in private, access-controlled storage. They are not publicly accessible and can only be viewed by the coach and linked parents for that player. We do not use player photos or videos for any purpose other than displaying them within the app.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be removed from our systems within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Request deletion of your child's information
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@coachconnectpro.app.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice in the app or sending an email to your registered address. Your continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at:

privacy@coachconnectpro.app
